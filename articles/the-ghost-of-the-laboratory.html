The ghost of the laboratory

Recovered from the original Avinash Holmes website archive.

It was a dark and chilly night, the kind of night where you would you want to stay inside and cozy up with a good book. 

However, I had work to do. I was in the middle of an experiment in the laboratory, and I needed a break. 

Therefore, I stepped outside to smoke a cigarette. The only source of light was the pale moon, casting an ominous glow on the deserted surroundings. 

The laboratory was located in a quiet area, and at that time of night, there was nobody around. 

As I sat in my chair under the tree and lit up my cigarette, shivering in the cold, I saw the automatic gate of the lab building open. 

At first, I assumed it was the security personnel doing their rounds but as the silhouette of the person drew closer, I realized it was someone else. 

The person was wearing an oversized coat, and their body outline was barely visible in the darkness. 

"Excuse me, can I borrow a cigarette?" the stranger asked in a low tone.
I handed him a cigarette, and he sat down beside me without saying a word. 

As he lit up his cigarette, I could only see the ember glowing and moving towards and away from his mouth.
The stranger's presence made me uneasy. There was something off about them, but I could not quite put my finger on it.

Then suddenly he asked me- what I was doing here at this time of the night, and I told him that I am in my last year of a PhD program. 

He further asked me what I plan to do after my PhD, and then I explained him my entire plan for the next five years. 

I expressed my concern about finding a postdoc position in the place I wanted.

The stranger listened intently and then offered me some advice.

"Don't worry about the future," the stranger whispered. "Live each day to the fullest."

His voice was so low that it sent chills down my spine. I could not shake the feeling that something was not right. 

Yet I went on telling him a story I read in my childhood. I told him how smart squirrels bury their food during the summer so they can survive in the winter.
The stranger chuckled and told me a story from his childhood about finding buried nuts in his backyard during the winter and dead squirrels in the summer.

I asked him how did squirrels die at the first place?
To which he replied, “I used to shoot them with my air gun, it was fun”.
Feeling disgusted, I finished my cigarette, and the stranger got up to return to the lab. 

Out of curiosity, I asked him which lab he was from, and he told me building 40, lab 402. 

And then suddenly, I felt a cold gust of wind, and the stranger disappeared into the darkness.
This was truly spooky. I don't beleiev in suoernatural or paranormal stuff so I too smashed my cigarette and walked back to my lab.

After working for an hour, I decided to go and meet this spooky stranger in his lab. This way I could also be sure that it was just a person and not that I was hallucinating.
I do not remember when was the last time I went to the fourth floor, maybe some 2-3 years ago. Maybe!
When I arrived on the fourth floor, at first I could not find the lab 402.
But then suddenly I heard the ding sound of the microwave Owen; I assumed this person must be in the student kitchen. 

Student kitchen was close to the emergency exit.
After reaching the student kitchen, I saw the stranger who was sitting in his oversized coat was eating a sandwich with headphones in his ears. 

He had long hairs covering his cheeks. Without even looking at me, he broke off a piece of the sandwich and offered it to me which I took without any hesitation, I really don't know why I did that.

All of sudden, I heard a loud thud of the emergency stairs door, a security guard flashed a torchlight on my face, asking what I was doing there in the dark. 

I was stunned to realize that the kitchen was empty, and the stranger was nowhere to be seen. It was only me sitting in the dark and there was a piece of rotten sandwitch covered in fungus at the kitchen table. 

I ran to the sink and puked. 

Feeling shaken and confused, I told him that I came here by mistake.
Security personnel was looking at my face in confusion, he thought I was drunk so he asked me to lock the lab and go home.
Without wasting much of my time, I locked my lab and left to home. 

As I got home, I had a few shots of whiskey to calm my nerves and eventually went to sleep.

The encounter with the stranger and the strange happenings in the lab building haunted me for a long time. 

I could not shake off the feeling that something was off, but I never found out what it was.

The end.
