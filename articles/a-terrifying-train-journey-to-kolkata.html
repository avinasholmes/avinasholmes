A Terrifying Train Journey to Kolkata

Recovered from the original Avinash Holmes website archive.

No matter what but do not eat anything from the hands of a stranger and remember to take care of your luggage.

Ok ma! I am not a kid anymore, Pinky told her mother who had been telling her list of do's and don'ts as it was her first maiden journey that too all alone.

The train began to move slowly and Pinky kept waving her hand until she could see her mother's face. 

This was her first journey where she was travelling all alone. The train from Lucknow to Kolkata takes around 18 hours to reach Howrah- the final station of this route. She has a reservation for third class air conditioner coach which was a lot better comparing to normal sleeper coaches.

22 A.. ..25B....27A....Yes...here it is 29 B.

The bottom bunk bed was her reserved berth. The small cubicle size compartment of this coach had total 8 berths and two windows. Three bunk beds in her front, three on her own side and two on her left. It appears that most of the beds were empty except one in her front where a pink school bag was lying on it. 

There was enough space under her bed so she pushed her trolley bag inside. As there were no passengers, she folded up the middle berth to make some space.

She downloaded a few movies on Netflix while she was at home. After train reaches Howrah, she will have to book a cab. From Howrah train station it takes only 10 minutes to reach her aunt's house.

Two days later she has to appear for a PhD entrance exam at Indian Institute of Science Education and Research-Kolkata. 

Rajdhani express is famous for it's punctuality, speed, safety and security. Also, unlike normal passenger trains, it stops at very few railway stations. 

Until Prayagraj railway station no passengers appeared to claim berths in her compartment. 

As the next train stop was after 2 hours, she plugged in her ear phones and started listening to her favorite playlist.

Is this some bad dream or its real, whatever it was for that moment Pinky was left frightened. 

There was an evil looking man around 45 in age, thick eyebrows, untrimmed grey and white beards, curly frizzy hairs, sunken eyes surrounded by black circles and this man was staring right at her face while she was lying on her bed.

WHO ARE YOU!!

She screamed in terror, quickly she got up and sat down on her bed.

The man didn't say anything, he returned to his berth where that pink bag was lying from the beginning. 

The playlist was still on so Pinky just turned off the music, her heartbeat was still fast and her brain was numb.

The speeding of the wheels while platform lights running backwards suggested that train was already running past the Mughal Sarai train station. She had no idea when she fell asleep.

In beginning she assumed that pink bag must have belonged to some girl until now. 

That man was acting as if nothing happened. He took out a water bottle and a tiffin box from that pink bag. Pinky realized that soon he might offer her food. She was just staring at that man who was busy taking out his food. 

The man turned at Pinky and said in his hollow voice- "I am not a great cook but I won't mind sharing one paratha with you!

He rolled one paratha and stretched his hand towards her.

Pinky didn't know what to do, she was totally bemused and sitting straight against the wall of her berth.

"Take it"

The man has a heavy and insisting voice. He too has a strange smile. 

Pinky was really scared, to avoid any problem she just took that paratha and went back to her bed.

Here.. you can wrap it in this piece of paper.

That man handed her a piece of old newspaper covered with translucent spots of cooking oil and some yellow turmeric stains. 

Without uttering a word she took that scrap of newspaper from his hand and wrapped that paratha inside.

She knew she shouldn't be eating that paratha at any cost. There has been plenty of such incidences in trains where people lost their belongings and sometimes even life after eating poisonous food offered by strangers. As being a girl she was more concerned about her safety than her belongings. Apart from these two there was nobody else in her coach, to her it was a bad omen.

All of sudden an idea clicked in her mind.

Excuse me! Could you please just keep an eye at my luggage until I return from the washroom?

The middle aged man just nodded his head in affirmation and continued eating. 

She took her handbag and swiftly she grabbed that paratha while leaving.

Out of curiosity she opened it to check what possibly he must have stuffed inside the paratha. She saw that inside paratha there was some pickle and smashed fried potatoes and nothing as such peculiar. Anyways, she threw that paratha inside the dustbin chained to the washroom door. To make sure, it can't be seen, she spreaded that newspaper wrap and dropped it above the paratha.

" Girl-24 jumped off the running train to save herself from getting raped, critically injured" 

The headline of that newspaper left her traumatized. Her heart was pounding loud and her brain was freezing. She had no idea what to do. She just kept staring at the headline.

Are you okay! 

Someone tapped her shoulder from behind.

She was already terrified and this touch left her petrified. Her kidneys just injected all the adrenaline into her vains. Her mouth was running dry, her legs were trembling. 

That same elderly man standing behind her. 

Somehow she mustered a little courage and to his question all she replied was one word- Yes!!

That man was still standing there. He has no smile on his face, he just continuously kept gawking at her face.

"I need to use washroom" the middle aged man said in a sonorous voice.

All of sudden she realized that she was blocking his way to washroom. 

She just moved away from the door. 

It was 1 AM, the corridor of her coach was lit by the chain of dim yellow light across the alley and the only sound she could hear was unremitting sound of the train running over the track. 

The light above the berth of that stranger was still on. 

What if she is overthinking? She questioned herself. 

But why was that old man gawking over her face while she was sleeping? That makes no sense! 

She was lost in lots of questions. She was alone and she was feeling vulnerable. 

She thought to watch some movie so that the stranger won't be interrupting her anymore.

She connected her bluetooth headphones and started pretending to be busy.

In a few minutes that stranger returned to his bed. 

So where are you going little girl? The middle aged man asked while spreading a bedsheet.

Pinky heard him but she intentionally ignored his question. She was checking his activities from the corner of her eyes while pretending to be wathcing a movie. For her safety she took out a pen from her side bag and kept it nearby. 

The middle aged man was smiling under the light right above his head.

She noticed that stranger took out a picture from his shirt pocket and he was staring at it. Sometimes he would look at the picture and sometimes towards her. 

Pinky's head was full of all the possible ugly scenarios and what she will do in order to protect her. 

Such a tense situation, she never thought her first train journey was going to be this awful.

Get up dear... Howrah station is arriving.

The old man was again standing right above her face but this time with a cup of tea.

Pinky had no idea when she slept at night.

She woke up hurriedly. She noticed that she was covered in a blanket. She knew who must have done this. She checked her trolley bag under her bed which was still there. 

It was 10 AM, the sun was shining bright. As it was day time and the train was running through the urban areas, she felt a bit relaxed. 

The middle aged man was eating biscuits while dipping them into the tea. 

Her phone battery was almost dead. The train was about to enter into Howrah station. The numbers of train tracks were increasing on her left side suggesting that platform was arriving on her right side.The screeching of the wheels assured that in a few minutes train will stop. 

Pinky was feeling alive once again. The middle aged man almost finished his tea.

He looked at her again and gave her a smile. She had no idea what to do so she faked a smile in return.

He collected his pink school bag and left his berth. All of sudden Pinky noticed that he forgot that photograph he was holding at night. She quickly ran to his bed and took that photograph.

It was a picture of a young girl. She had a face just like her. 

Pinky took out her trolley bag and ran to the door where her must be standing. 

Excuse me! Pinky tapped on his shoulder.

The middle aged man turned towards her and smiled.

I think you forgot your photograph, Pinky said while giving him that picture she found on his berth.

The middle aged man quickly clutched that photograph from her hand and for a minute he just kept looking at that photograph. After a long pause he kept that photograph back into his shirt pocket. 

Thank you very much sweetheart!! He said with teary eyes.

There was a sudden sense of guilt taking over Pinky's mind.

That stranger was still looking at her face. It was obvious from his expressions that he was trying to hold back his tears.

The train stopped. There was a huge crowd over the platform.

In his stammering voice he said- My daughter Pragya was just like you, last year she couldn't qualify her intermediate exams....Soo.... he paused for a while and with a great effort he said- she was my only child.

He took a deep breath, his eyes were full of tears. He quickly grabbed his bag and waived his hand in goodbye. 

Pinky was still standing at the door holding her trolley bag. She kept looking at that man until she lost him in the crowd. 

The clamoring sound of the tea-sellers, newspaper hawkers, announcements over the loudspeaker everything went queit for that moment. 

Pinky just took her trolley bag and started to look for a way out to the train station.

Her mind was clear of all the doubts she had earlier. She just kept walking among the crowd thinking about that man who's face would have terrified her forever had she not found that photograph.
