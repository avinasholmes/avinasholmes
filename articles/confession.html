English PoemMay 25, 2021

Confession

Recovered from the original Avinash Holmes website archive.

If I told you everything,

All my flaws and all my sins,

Who I am and what I think,

Everything, every trivial thing,

If I showed you my weaker side,

How I am afraid when comes the night,

How I don't like being alone,

And I worry too much for those who are gone,

what If I asked you to look into my eyes,

Eyes where all my insecurities hide,

If I told you I am not always right,

And I too want someone to hold me tight,

What if I told you I am not the one,

I lost more hearts than hearts I won, 

And after all this,

What If I told you that you are my world,

My morning breeze, sunshine when its cold,

If I told you that you give me hope,

You give me reasons among the nopes,

You draw smile at my gloomy face,

That when you leave me my poor heart aches,

After I confess all of this

Now when you my every bit,

Would you stay until dawn and dusk,

Would you love me even when I am not the best?
