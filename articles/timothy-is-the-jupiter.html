As it was a special occasion for someone who got all her attention, it took her more time to dress up than usual. Anyways, dressed in a beautiful accordion-pleated skirt with a ruffle top she made her way into this beautiful restaurant located right in the heart of the New York city.

Oh yes! Timothy Jr. was a 28 years old, young, dashing, well dressed, charming, gentleman. Apart from the fact that he was rich and came in a roaring Lamborghini, he was chivalrous, humble and and an extremely polite handsome man. To add a bit more, he carries a sweetest smile and an irresistible chiseled-jawline which of course was the first reason Sandra swiped him right on the Tinder.

The food was served and for Sandra, he ordered the best dish available in restaurant- the white truffle pasta along with the red wine. 

And not to forget, he handed her a beautiful bouquet of 50 fresh roses and a box of chocolates. Definitely, he was the perfect man Sandra has been looking for years.

He poured some wine for Sandra as she kept looking at his face and his shining eyes.

Beep beep...

A few notifications from tinder just popped up on his phone screen.

Excuse me!! Timothy picked up his phone to check the notifications.

Sandra wouldn't mind anything after all she was with the most handsome and chivalrous guy sitting right in front of her.

Timothy kept smiling while typing apparently a reply on his phone, after replying he kept his phone back on the dinner table.

The screen was still on.

Excuse me! Timothy called the waitress who was just passing by.

Yes sir! Is everything good?

Oh yes! Perfect, the food is awesome, Could you please show me which side to the restroom?

Sure sir! It's straight and then to the left, the waitress answered.

Pardon me for a moment dear Sandra I will be right back in a minute, sorry! he smiled.

Goodness! He is sizzling, Sandra smiled and just nodded her head in approval.

As soon Timothy left for the washroom, a few more notifications popped up on his phone screen which was lying unlocked on the dinner table.

Sandra was completely lost in his thoughts until now. She looked at his phone and even though she knew that she shouldn't be sneaking into his phone, she realized that the notifications on his phone were from Tinder. 

Curiously, Sandra turned his phone on her side and clicked on one of those notifications.

Her eyes were left open in shock. 

"You are the moon of my life" 

That was the last line he typed before he left to the washroom.

He was hitting on other girls while he was already on a date with her.

To add some more shock, she noticed that Timothy has forwarded same line to some 20-30 more girls down in the chat.

Fucking unbelievable! she muttered, angrily.

Fuming Sandra collected her bag and left the restaurant in rage.

Before she exited, she left a 50$ bill on the table and she wrote a note on a napkin.

Timothy Jr., You should have told me that your actual name is Jupiter and not Timothy Jr. as the fact Jupiter has 79 moons, good luck to you but I am not going to add up in your list of moons.

PS: you should have never sent me that same shit line which you have just send to 20 other girls on Tinder.

F### you!

This was her first beautiful date and it ended so awfully.

Probably Timothy was collecting moons whereas Sandra was again left in disappointment.
