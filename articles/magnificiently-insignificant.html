Magnificiently insignificant

Recovered from the original Avinash Holmes website archive.

Professor Sharma, one of the most prominent Indian physicists of his time, well known for his splendid work on dark matter, black holes and the discovery of wormholes was about to arrive. Auditorium hall of the Lorento college was tightly packed. First few rows were occupied by the distinguished professors, journalists followed by students from different colleges and universities. 

The atmosphere inside the college campus has turned into a festive season. The college gate was decorated with welcome posters and festoons. 

How much time is left in his arrival?

Dr Ramakant, the principal of the college asked his secretary.

Sir, in about seven to eight minutes his carvan should be at the college gate, Mr. Shantaram, the secretary, replied.

Great! Make sure the lunch is served on time right after his lecture is over. Also, tell the head of departments to be present there.

Sure sir! Shantaram took a note.

Dr. Ramakant called his chauffeur and asked him to pick him up from his office. He instructed him to accompany him to the college gate. 

Approximately after five minutes of waiting the carvan appeared. Dr. Ramakant greeted Professor with a bouquet of flowers. The moment was filled with sound and flashes of the camera. 

Professor Sharma asked Dr. Ramakant to take a ride with him until the auditorium. 

*****

"Dear all finally the wait is over," the head of the physics department- Dr Kamble announced holding a bright smile on his face. 

Everyone please give a standing ovation and a round of applause to our chief guest, nobel laureate, honourable professor Dr Jayram Sharma.

The auditorium hall was full of claps as Professor Sharma entered the auditorium gate along with Dr. Ramakant and three of his security guards.

Right after his entry the head of the physics department fecilitated him with a college memento and a shawl. Again the auditorium was siezed by the flashes and the camera shutter sound.

Professor Sharma waved his hand towards attendees and requested them to sit down.

After reading a long list of his achievements and a brief summary of his astounding research work, Dr. Ramakant finally invited professor over the podium. 

Professor Sharma who was around 68 in his age was a tall and fit looking person. In a white three piece suit and grey hairs, he was fullfilling every definition of how one would imagine a professor by looks. To mention, nobody has ever seen him smiling but people say that he has a good sense of humor.

He asked Dr Kamble to help him with connecting projector.

"Human Ignorance & The Size Of The Universe"

Professor Jayram Sharma

Lorento college, 2021"

This was the title of his talk.

Within a minute a pin drop silence prevailed inside the auditorium. The lights inside the hall were turned off except a few small bulbs embedded in the stairs of the alley, a green neon sign board showing emergency exit and a white bulb right above the podium where professor was standing. 

To everyone who came here to attend my today's lecture and to hear me talking about the crude science, I am sorry to disappoint you all, but today I am going to talk more about philosophy. 

Prof. Sharma turned towards the white board and pressed the button to move on to the next slide.

What is life?

That was the title of his next slide.

He turned towards the audiences and began speaking in his charismatic voice.

I think a very few of you would know that until my bachelor's I studied biology. When I started working in a lab during summer internship, it was my first time when I was left mesmerized after I saw tiny transparent vials storing the building blocks of the DNA molecule. I was thrilled when I found building blocks of protein, carbohydrates, RNA too were stored in the shelves of the refrigerator. 

To me it was like finding lego blocks of the life and now all was left for me to create anything alive.

but you know what kicked in my mind?

A question and that was- "Are these things even alive? 

And I am pretty sure that even a potato would know the answer- of course not! 

But you do realize that these same things inside our bodies are working day and night, storing information, using that information encoded in DNA to make tools like protein to copy and make more DNA or make more protein and other essential set of items required for our survival.

Collectively speaking, everything I saw inside shelves was definitely not alive as per the definition of biology. But then we should ask, if those chemicals inside the vials are not alive then everything that makes us too is just dead as dirt. If not then everything we call dead is simply alive!

Everyone inside the hall was enchanted and if you would pay a little attention then you could hear the sound of people biting their nails.

Prof. Sharma had a glass of water and continued speaking.

Well, as far as I understand I believe that everything is alive in this universe. Everything is working, everything is in motion and everything is breathing just like us. The very elements that makes us are found in every corner of this galaxy. As the universe has got no eyes to gaze upon it's breathtaking beauty, for that reason universe has created us using the same dust its made of. In gist, we among every living creation are the embodiment of this very universe and through our eyes and experiences this universe is gazing upon itself in bewilderment. Everyday it is discovering new things about itself and then after a period everything disintegrates and we go back to universe to serve some other purposes. 


Magnificently insignificant!

Third slide was in progress.

The very question that bothered Buddha and forced him to leave his beautiful kingdom still keeps bothering many of us and has bothered me too, lately.

What is the purpose of life?

Well I have figured out that life doesn't have any purpose and it's we who keep pushing this idea that it must be having some purpose. Similarly, like when we don't understand why this universe exists and why we are here our brain comes up with the existence of some supreme being we call- God.

Look around, there are thousands of species of flora and fauna and none of them are worried about finding the purpose of their lives except following one single inherent principle given to every life, no matter how primitive life form is and that is, to reproduce and pass on the evolutionary information collected in their DNA. 

Does anyone here think that nature and evolution cares about your precious memories or astounding discoveries including my own achievements or my publications? 

Definitely Not! One day like everyone else I too will die. We all spend days and years trying to give a best badge of purpose to this life because we have trained our mind to look for complicated scenrios, questions which are the reasons we never feel content.

Anyways, you must be wondering what's with this word magnificently insignificant!

He popped a button and a small animation video began.

Starting from the heart of an atom zooming out from the atomic realms where distances were in femtometers, which is one trillionth of the size of a millimeter, started shrinking to DNA building block- a nucleotide, then to cell's nucleus then to a human epithelial cell which is on an average is around 100 micrometer. 

The cell was followed by human eye then size comparison of a human to Eiffel tower to Mount Everest to the Earth followed by Jupiter, Sun, Solar system, black hole in the heart of our milky way galaxy then the Saraswati supercluster of galaxies which extends to 1.2 Gigaparsec equivalent to 3262000000 light years and then the last image was observable universe and finally animation ended with human arrogance and ignorance written underquotes.

The video stopped.

It was obvious that everyone in hall was enthralled.

Did you notice that how infinitesimally inconspicious we are in comparison to everything else out there in space but yet everyday we walk with our gigantic egos, prodigious arrogance and towering ignorance!

Professor Sharma looked at his wrist watch and realized that he was getting late for the meeting he has an hour later.

My apologies, but I must stop this presentation here as I have a meeting to attend.

The auditorium lights were turned on.

People who were spellbound till now started clapping in unison.

A few students from school requested professor for his autograph but professor was running late. He just looked away and walked out of the hall with Dr. Ramakant. 

The doorkeeper opened the door showing him way inside the dining area.

The warm and delicious meal was waiting at the dining table, the room was full with appetizing aroma.

As professor Sharma sat on a special chair designated only for him, he was followed by the head of departments and Dr Ramakant sitting right beside him. 

Mr. Pal and his catering team of six people were assigned for the job to serve food. Mr. Pal who was a bit old around 64 in age had been the cook of Lorento college canteen since he was 28, undoubtedly the best chef.

Mr. Pal started serving delicacies and he began with the head of physics department Dr. Kamble.

After finishing serving to all eight head of departments, he finally moved to professor Sharma who had been staring at him since he started with Dr. Kamble. Although he had been conversing with Principal Ramakant his eyes had been following Mr. Pal.

Mr. Pal took a ladle full of malai kofta (a food dish) and gently poured it into a bowl. 

Professor this is my a special dish I made especially for you, he said. 

Nobody expected this but as soon he leaned over to place the bowl beside professor Sharma, it slipped through his hands.

The white new coat of professor Sharma was now fully drenched in yellow curry.

Everyone was in shock. Dr. Ramakant asked for a towel immediately.

Professor Sharma was shaking in anger. He was staring at Mr. Pal with his clenched jaw and Mr Pal has his legs trembling in fear. He realized that today was the last day of his career.

What a stupid person! professor Sharma screamed in anger. 

First you started serving with everyone else as if I was not the chief guest of this event and then how dare you pour the food over my coat. Do you have any idea how important is my next meeting? His deafening voice was echoing inside the dining hall.

All of sudden Sharma pushed back his chair and stood up. 

Mr. Pal was sweating and with folded hands he was just standing there. He was not able to look up into professor's eye.

Had this guy ever been to a college or even to a school? Sharma mockingly asked to Dr. Ramakant, the principle of the Lorento college.

I guess not because then he wouldn't be retiring here as a cook, isn't it! 

Everyone in dining hall was shocked and quiet.

Professor, I am extremely sorry for his behavior, I will arrange you a coat right away. Dr. Ramakant looked at Dr. Kamble, he immediately rushed to find a new coat for him.

There were no signs that he was going to calm down anytime soon.

He took out his curry coated coat and tossed it over the chair he was sitting before.

Angrily he ran to the door followed by everyone else. 

Ramakant! I have never been humiliated like this ever in my life. 

Didn't he know who I am? I mean did you notice how he started serving food to everyone else while I was sitting right there.

Dr. Ramakant who has been listening to his incessant rant finally said- Sir, please accept my apologies on his behalf, I will see what can be done in his case. 

"Fire him! I would suggest that you better fire him if you don't want any further embarrassments" Sharma replied while entering into his car. 

After all these many years of appreciation, today marked the most undesirable day in the life of Mr. Pal. Everyone else has left except Pal who was still sitting alone in the dining hall. His eyes were full of tears, he was looking at the chair where professor Sharma was sitting a moment before. 

How could I do such a silly mistake like this! 

Mr. Pal..!! someone called his name,

He quickly wiped off his face and turned towards the door where one of his catering team member- Ramesh was standing at the door.

Yes! Who needs me now? Holding a lump in his throat Mr. Pal asked.

Dr. Ramakant wants you to appear in his office right now.

The expressions at the face of Ramesh were worrisome, as if he knew what was coming next.

Mr. Pal followed Ramesh and asked him to lock the door of the dining hall.

With heavy steps he slowly walked towards the principal office. He realized that his retirement was coming within 2-3 years but like everyone he too wished to retire respectfully. 

Before knocking at the door he again wiped off his face on his sleeves and tried to hide his tears which were still shining bright.

Knock knock!

Come in! Dr. Ramakant answered.

Hanging his head down in regret Pal entered the principle's chamber.

Dr Ramakant who was checking something in his computer paused his work and asked him to sit down.

Mr. Pal I guess you understand why you are here? Ramakant enquired while pulling out the drawer of the table.

Pal nodded his head without looking up.

After a minute of silence Dr. Ramakant pulled out a big envelope from his drawer.

As expected Pal realized that it was time for him to pack up and leave this campus forever. The campus where he has spent half of his life, leaving it in such a disdainful way was something he never imagined.

Would you please open this envelope for me? Ramakant asked while he slid the envelope in the direction Pal was sitting.

After a long pause Pal took a deep breath, collected that big brown envelope and carefully opened the top flap.

As obvious, his hand felt the presence of a few papers wrapped inside the envelope.

Take it out! Dr. Ramakant who appeared to be eagerly waiting said while holding back his smile.

Without a delay Mr. Pal emptied the envelope right on the table. 

A few cheques along with a folded paper appears to be a handwritten letter fell down.

Pal who was expecting an official termination letter now had no idea what exactly was happening.

There were 8 bank cheques right in front of him. Every cheque has a value of ₹10,000 marked with a signature of every head of the department of the Lorento college. The ninth cheque which was signed by Dr. Ramakant himself was written off for ₹ 20,000.

Mr. Pal who's face was showing all signs of penitence a moment before was now showing that he was left dumbfounded. 

Sir, what is this for? Mr. Pal muttered in confusion.

Just read the letter, Ramakant asked with a smile on his face.

He opened that letter without wasting a second.

The letter read-

"Dear Mr. Pal,

As Professor Sharma taught us today that human arrogance and ignorance is far bigger than the whole universe, we have decided to make a little amendment in his statement. We want to thank you for selflessly serving this college for these many years. We also want to apologize for the disrespect you faced today. Also, please accept our sincere apologies that we did not thank you earlier for your immense contribution in making every conference a successful event by its end. We thank you for adding taste, love and affection in our food.

We wish you a good health and time in this campus for next many more years.

Yours sincerely

Dr. Ramakant and the undersigned HODs"

The tears were rolling down his cheeks.

Mr. Pal didn't know what to say. Dr. Ramakant moved to him and did pat on his shoulder. 

Pal, I am sorry for what happened today. I remember that you told me you too wanted to become a scientist but today I am telling you proudly that choosing hotel management was your best decision. 

Thank you sir! Mr. Pal said while wiping off his tears.

Pal, I also want to tell you a secret only if you never pass it to my wife.

Okay sir! He replied innocently.

Mr. Pal, all these years I only ate my lunch in canteen and that's because you cook better than me or my wife.

But sir I have seen you bringing tiffin for your lunch eveyday! Mr. Pal exclaimed in surprise,

Oh! that I give to Shantaram- my secretary.

The room was full of giggles.

There were tears of happiness shining in the eyes of Mr. Pal. 

Pal I will be leaving home in ten minutes so if you want I can drop you home.

Sure sir! I will go and collect my bag from the canteen, he said. 

Have you ever seen the smiling face of that person who gets recognition and respect after years of unrecognized hard work? 

Yes exactly, that same smile was there on his face.

Pal, I will be in my office do let me know when you are back. Dr. Ramakant smiled.

This beautiful gesture by college was something which Pal was never going to forget. 

Also, Dr. Ramakant never felt this content in his whole life. The lesson Professor Jayram Sharma taught seems to have changed many lives except his own. 

In Professor Jayram's world human arrogance and ignorance were bigger than the whole universe but in Dr. Ramakant and Mr. Pal's life kindness and affection was way more bigger than everything else.
