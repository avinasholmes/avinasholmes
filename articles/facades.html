English PoemFebruary 16, 2025

Facades

Recovered from the original Avinash Holmes website archive.

It’s all about facades,
It’s all about masks.
There are faces we must hide,
Before those who despise us,
For they find joy in our sorrow.
And before those who would loathe us,
If they saw us as too happy, too free.

You might think it’s real—
But it’s a facade.
They let the world believe they thrive,
That they have achieved beyond others.
But one day, when you catch them alone,
Sitting beneath a quiet tree,
Away from their bright phone screens,
In a fleeting moment of reflection—
They begin to remove their masks.

And there, you see their true face,
Etched with wounds—
Carved by others’ opinions,
Shaped by expectations,
Scarred by their own relentless race.
You notice how their shoulders droop,
How, for just a moment,
They set aside the weight of pretense.

And then—just as quickly—
The masks return, the facade restored.
They come running, laughing,
Dancing behind the illusion,
Carrying the burden
That has become a way of life—
A life in its essence.
