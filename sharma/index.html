What does the score mean?

Scores of 55 and above indicate a bullish experimental signal; scores of 45 and below indicate a bearish signal. Scores between those thresholds are near neutral. A score of 80 is not an 80% probability. The score alone is not a buy, sell, or short instruction.

What happens when I refresh?

The page requests the latest available snapshot. A new forecast window appears only when a newer calculation is available. Historical or stale snapshots keep their original times. A refresh never manufactures a fresh forecast.

Which information is included?

Price/volume scores and separately collected news context have independent timestamps. GDELT organization matches are incomplete and unverified. Social search may be unavailable. News tone and social posts are not yet inputs to the formula. Investor identities and order-book pressure are unavailable. Sector membership comes from a secondary-source constituent snapshot.

How will predictive value be tested?

The initial replay compares the fixed model with momentum, RSI reversal, and always-up baselines. It includes a one-minute execution delay and assumed costs of 0, 10 and 20 basis points. Its two sessions are not a holdout. The next step is prospective timestamped collection and evaluation across more trading sessions, with calibrated targets and uncertainty.
